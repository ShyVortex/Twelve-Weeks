About

What is Twelve Weeks?

A free, fan-made multiplayer mod for The Last of Us Part II Remastered on PC. It brings back clan-based team multiplayer in the spirit of the 2013 multiplayer: pick a side, play 4v4 matches, and keep your clan alive for twelve weeks.

Does it cost anything?

No. The mod is free. You need your own copy of The Last of Us Part II Remastered on Steam.

Is it official?

No. Twelve Weeks is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Sony Interactive Entertainment or Naughty Dog.

Does it include game files?

No. The mod patches the game you already own, on your own PC.

The Part I maps need your own copy of The Last of Us Part I. Press INSTALL FROM MY PART I in the launcher once; after that you can uninstall Part I and the maps stay.

Can I play on Steam Deck or Linux?

Yes, with the Linux launcher (an AppImage).

  1. Start The Last of Us Part II once from Steam, until its menu, then close it.
  2. On Steam Deck, switch to Desktop mode.
  3. Make the AppImage executable and run it.
  4. Press UPDATE, then PLAY.

No Steam launch option is needed.

Can I add Twelve Weeks to my Steam library?

Yes. The launcher's ADD TO STEAM button adds it as a non-Steam game, with its own artwork.

How do I update?

The launcher updates itself and the mod.

Can I still play the single-player campaign?

Yes. Press SWITCH TO SP in the launcher, and SWITCH TO MP to come back.

How do I uninstall?

Use the UNINSTALL button in the launcher options.

Playing

What modes are there?
  • Supply Raid: bleed out the enemy team's reinforcements.
  • Interrogation: interrogate downed enemies to find their safe, then rob it.
How many players per match?

Up to 8: four WLF against four Seraphites.

What are clans and the twelve weeks?

Your clan lives on what you bring home. Each match is one day. Keep your survivors fed and healthy, grow the camp, and see who is still standing after twelve weeks. Missions, events and journal entries come along the way.

How do I pick a faction?

You choose WLF or Seraphites once, for your clan. The choice lasts until your clan dies or survives long enough to win.

How do loadouts work?

You have 13 loadout points. Pick one of the 8 pre-made classes or build up to 6 custom loadouts. You can edit them in the game or in the launcher's GEAR UP tab.

Which weapons and skills are in?

Every weapon and skill from the original PvP mode of The Last of Us and The Last of Us Remastered, plus a few extras. Parts earned in a match upgrade weapons or buy armour and other purchasables.

How does melee work?

It's the single-player melee rebuilt for multiplayer, made as fair as possible for both sides, with dodges, counters and breakout prompts. Still a work in progress.

How many maps are there?

14 right now: 12 from Part II and 2 from Part I (Bookstore and Plaza).

What are Part I maps?

Maps converted from The Last of Us Part I campaign, such as Bookstore and Plaza. They are optional. In the game's MP options you can choose to always include them in your map votes; you then only queue with other Part I owners.

Is controller supported?

Yes. Every keyboard feature also works on a pad.

Which languages?

English and Polish for now. All languages the game supports are planned.

Connection and servers

Where are the servers located?

One in Europe and one in NA West.

Why do I need to have Steam open?

Your Steam ID is your Twelve Weeks account, so Steam has to run while you play.

Are private matches available?

Not yet, they are planned. You can make a party in the lobby's FRIENDS tab, and the party queues into matches together.

Can I host my own server?

Yes. The GitHub repository has the server code and setup docs.

Is there an anti-cheat system?

Yes. Automatic flags and player reports from the launcher catch cheaters. Obvious cheating is banned instantly; anything else goes to an admin, who reviews the match and decides. The official servers only accept the official build, so modified forks cannot join.

Troubleshooting

The game crashes or will not start.
  1. In the launcher, run Verify / Repair (Settings > Files).
  2. Start the game through the launcher, not through Steam directly.
  3. If it still fails, send a bug report (below).
How do I report a bug?

Use REPORT A BUG in the launcher. Describe what happened and when, and add the map and mode. The launcher shows exactly which log files it sends, then gives you a report code (R-xxxx). Post that code in the Discord.

The launcher says the update server is unreachable.

PLAY still works with the mod version you have installed. Try again later for updates.

Where are the logs?

In the launcher, Settings > OPEN LOGS. The main game log is t2r-ext.log in the game folder.
