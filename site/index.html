01

Fight

4v4 team fights between the WLF and the Seraphites. Bleed out the enemy's reinforcements in Supply Raid, or interrogate their downed players to find the enemy safe and rob it in Interrogation.

  • Tense team matches with scarce ammo and limited lives
  • Classic arsenal: every weapon and skill from the original PvP mode of The Last of Us and The Last of Us Remastered
  • Special executions, contextual takedowns and more to finish the enemy in style
02

Craft

Loot parts and crafting ingredients from boxes and the environment, then turn them into the tools you need. Burn your enemies with molotovs, blow them up with pipe bombs, or plant one to catch them off guard.

  • Molotovs, bombs, smoke bombs and shivs to use against your enemies
  • Med kits to recover from a bad fight, or to escape one that isn't going your way
  • Parts earned in the match upgrade your weapons, buy armour or any purchasable of your choice
03

Brawl

Close the gap and settle it by hand. Melee reads from both sides: swing, dodge, counter and finish with weapons from bats and pipes to machetes and hatchets. Still a work in progress, so expect some inconsistencies.

  • Single-player melee, rebuilt for players on both ends
  • Dodges, grabs and executions on downed enemies
  • Weapons that break, so make every hit count
04

Survive

Your clan lives on what you bring home. Twelve weeks, one day per match: keep them fed and healthy, grow the camp, and see who is still standing at the end.

  • A camp that grows or starves with your results
  • Missions, events and journal entries along the way
  • Pick your faction and wear its colours
